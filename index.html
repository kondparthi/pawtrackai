What is collected

Dog photos, vaccination, sterilisation and health entries, ward, and who made each entry.

Who can access it

Residents see a dog's care status. NGOs, vets and the municipality see the operational record for their area.

Why locations are restricted

Public views show ward level only, so precise locations cannot be used to target or remove dogs.

How records are verified

Entries stay provisional until an NGO or vet confirms them. Every entry carries its author and date.

How reports are handled

A citizen report becomes a task for the ward team, and its outcome is recorded against the dog.

How results are reported

Pilot metrics are published after the review, including what did not work.
